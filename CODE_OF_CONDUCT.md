# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in any form.
- Keep discussion constructive and on topic; critique code and design, not contributors.
- Report problems privately to the maintainers (see SECURITY.md for contact). Maintainers may remove content or contributors that violate these expectations.
