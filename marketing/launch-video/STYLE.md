# Style guide extracted from the reference video

Source: the Pocketsflow launch video (x.com/achxvi/status/2105971622247067690), 38 s, 1920 × 1080. Frames were sampled every 4 s; this is the grammar we borrow, not the content.

## Structure
1. Hook on white: the question builds word by word ("Got / something / to …").
2. Brand reveal on a saturated brand blue: wordmark, then a three-line headline whose last line has a serif italic accent ("that *you deserve.*") underlined with a hand-drawn stroke.
3. Feature chapters, one per beat cluster. Each chapter has its own pale tint (lavender, pink, blue, green, cream, mint) and the same layout: mono kicker top left, headline left, product card right.
4. Recap grid: all chapters as small tiles, each getting a checkmark in sequence.
5. End card on white: large wordmark, tagline with the same italic accent, primary CTA button plus URL, mono stats line ("95,000+ people · 180+ countries · no monthly fee").

## Typography
- Headline: heavy neutral grotesk, two to three lines, the final word set in a serif italic in the chapter colour ("upsells.", "paid.", "taxes.").
- Kicker: small mono, lower case, prefixed with `//`, e.g. `// one-click upsells · email marketing`.
- HUD: tiny mono strip at the very top: section number and name on the left, brand plus running timecode on the right.

## Product
- Real-looking UI cards with soft shadows and 16 px radii, shown large on the right half and often bleeding off-frame.
- A cursor performs one meaningful action per chapter (clicks Apple Pay, fills a field); the UI responds on the click.
- Coloured chips appear one by one on the beat ("Card", "Apple Pay", "Google Pay", "PayPal").

## Colour
- One saturated brand colour (blue) used for the reveal and CTA; chapter tints are very light washes of distinct hues; ink is near-black.

## Motion
- Snappy entrances (expo out), long holds, hard cuts between chapters, a counter that ticks up, checkmarks popping in sequence.
- The pace is about 4 s per chapter, so roughly one bar of 120 BPM per idea pair.

## What we change for Open Reports
- No mascot: the hero object is the document itself. Paper pages, tables and labels are our characters.
- Chapter colours map to the product's own status palette and accent so the video looks like the app.
